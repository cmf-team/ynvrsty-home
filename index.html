YNVRSTY

Rigorous education for people

YNVRSTY is an international, tuition-free education platform for people with strong technical skills and high ethical standards. Our first school — the Center of Mathematical Finance — has been training quants, researchers and engineers since 2022. More schools are on the way.

/ 01 — What we stand for
01

Tuition-free, always

Talent is distributed evenly across the world; opportunity is not. Every programme we run is free to the people who earn a place on it.

02

Taught by practitioners

Instructors with ten or more years in the field, building real projects alongside students rather than lecturing at them.

03

Research that ships

Cohorts publish working code and papers under an open licence. Our portfolio is public — read it before you decide to apply.
